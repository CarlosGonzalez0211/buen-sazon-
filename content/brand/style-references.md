# Style references (shared by the owner, Oct 2026)

Four posts from other Ciudad Juárez restaurants, kept as inspiration for the *feel* of El Buen Sazón's designs.
We borrow techniques, never wording, layouts line for line, or logos.

## What each one does

| Reference | What to notice |
|---|---|
| **Flor de Arena: "Sabores que unen"** | Warm single-hue background with a faint illustrated texture. A huge condensed headline stacked on two lines in two colors (cream-yellow, then white). A handwritten script line underneath ("Momentos que se disfrutan."). Small logo top center, one small address line at the bottom. The plate enters from the right edge and is cropped by the frame. |
| **Flor de Arena: carousel slide 2** | A moody, close photo of a gloved hand garnishing a plate. A strip of the first slide's orange peeks in at the left edge, so the slides feel like one piece. |
| **New York New York: "¿Truco o antojo?"** | A playful pun tied to the season. Speckled orange paper texture. Bold condensed white headline, a tiny subline. Die-cut sticker elements (ghost, pumpkin, bats) with a thick white outline, plus little sparkles. Logo small, bottom right. |
| **Rompecabezas Café: "Nuevos chilaquiles"** | A real photo of the dishes on the table, in context. A big white script headline overlapping the photo. Handwritten labels with hand-drawn arrows pointing at the food (Verdes, Crema, Queso, Rojos, Cebolla). Logo top center, address at the bottom. 4-slide carousel. |

## Patterns across all four
1. **Mixed type**: a heavy headline paired with a handwritten script accent. Two voices on one flyer.
2. **Left-aligned or edge-bleeding layouts**: elements are cropped by the frame, not centered in a box.
3. **Sticker treatment**: cutouts and props with a thick white die-cut outline, a little rotated, sometimes with sparkles.
4. **Texture or real photography**, not flat color. Photos are in context (table, hands, light).
5. **Plays on words** instead of plain labels ("¿Truco o antojo?", "Sabores que unen").
6. **Small logo, off-center** (top or a corner), with a single small info line at the bottom.
7. **Hand-annotated food** (arrows and script labels) as the way to explain a dish.
8. **Carousel continuity**: a shared color or edge strip across slides.

## How this meets our brand kit
- The kit fixes **Lilita One + Nunito** and the red / yellow / green palette. Reference 1 and 3 use a *condensed* grotesque, reference 3 and 4 a *script*. Neither is in the kit yet.
- Our current flyers are centered and use a large centered logo. The references suggest asymmetric layouts and a small corner logo.
