# El Buen Sazón · Brand Kit

## Colors

| Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `primary` (Rojo) | `#AC020B` | Main brand color. Backgrounds, headlines on light backgrounds, rules, buttons |
| `accent` (Amarillo) | `#FACC17` | Highlights. Backgrounds, headlines on red/green, prices, callouts |
| `deep` (Verde) | `#023C26` | Dark brand color. Backgrounds and all dark text |
| `surface` (Blanco) | `#FFFFFF` | Light backgrounds and text on red/green |
| `text-dark` | `#023C26` | Body text on yellow and white (same as `deep`) |
| `text-light` | `#FFFFFF` | Body text on red and green |

```css
:root {
  --primary: #AC020B;
  --accent: #FACC17;
  --deep: #023C26;
  --surface: #FFFFFF;
  --text-dark: #023C26;
  --text-light: #FFFFFF;
}
```

Don't use pure black for text. Use `deep` instead. Black appears only inside the logo.

## What goes on what

| Background | Headline | Body text | Rule/line | Logo file |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Red `#AC020B` | White or yellow | White | Yellow | White-outline version |
| Yellow `#FACC17` | Red | Dark green | Red | Original |
| Green `#023C26` | Yellow | White | Yellow | White-outline version |
| White `#FFFFFF` | Red | Dark green | Red | Original |

Contrast (4.5:1 or higher is readable): white on red 7.6, yellow on red 5.0, white on green 12.5, yellow on green 8.2, red on yellow 5.0, green on yellow 8.2, red on white 7.6, green on white 12.5.

Never use: white on yellow (1.5), red on green (1.7), or yellow on white. They are hard to read.

## Backgrounds: which to use when

- **Icon pattern, bold colors (tonal):** Promos and announcements with a short headline and little text.
- **Icon pattern, white with colored icons:** Clean posts that still need some brand feel, such as hours or menu highlights.
- **Solid color:** Text-heavy posts (menus, schedules) or posts with busy photos.
- **Two-color split or diagonal:** Posts with two parts, such as breakfast and lunch, or AM and PM.
- **Spotlight circle or arch:** One dish photo as the hero.

## Typography

- **Headlines:** Lilita One, all caps. About 120–165 px on a 1080 px wide post.
- **Body and details:** Nunito Bold. Dish names and text about 38 px, subheads about 44 px. Don't go below 34 px.
- Both are free on Google Fonts and support accents and ¡ ¿ Ñ.
- Keep headlines to one or two short lines.

## Logo

- Use the original-color logo on white or yellow. Use the white-outline logo on red, green or photos.
- Don't recolor, stretch, rotate or add effects to the logo.
- Leave clear space around it at least equal to the height of the "S" in "Sazón."
- Keep the logo at least 300 px wide on a 1080 px post, so the small tagline stays readable.

## Formats

- Post size: 1080 × 1296 px (5:6).
- Background and layout templates are in the Design canvas.
