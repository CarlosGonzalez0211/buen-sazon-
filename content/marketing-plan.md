# El Buen Sazón · Plaza Juárez — Social marketing plan

Scope: Instagram + Facebook, one location (Plaza Juárez), Spanish, casual and inviting.
Cadence: 3 posts a week (Tue / Thu / Sat, noon). Everything is approved in Telegram before it goes out.

> Items marked **[confirm]** are assumptions. Please correct them before captions are written.

## 1. Positioning (one sentence)

"La comida de casa, hecha con sazón, en tu centro comercial": homestyle Mexican breakfasts, caldos, antojitos and
platillos at Plaza Juárez, served with a friendly, family feel.

What the photo library backs up: caldos (res, ranchero), chilaquiles, enchiladas, chiles rellenos, tacos, tostadas,
club sandwich, desayuno deluxe, café. The hero is generous, home-style plates, not fine dining.

## 2. Who we talk to **[confirm]**
- Mall shoppers and families at lunch / weekend (Plaza Juárez traffic).
- Office and retail workers nearby: quick, filling lunch.
- Breakfast crowd: chilaquiles, desayuno deluxe, café.
- Moms, dads and grandparents: the "come together" occasions.

## 3. Content pillars (what we post when there is NO promotion)

Rotate so no two posts in a row look the same. Target mix over a month (12–13 posts):

| Pillar | Share | What it is | Template |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Plato estrella** | 35% | One dish, hero cutout, short craving line | Hero dish on circle/arch |
| **2. Rituales de la semana** | 20% | A repeating, named habit: "Martes de caldo", "Sábado de chilaquiles", "Café y buen sazón". Builds routine without a discount | Headline + dish |
| **3. Detrás del sabor** | 15% | Kitchen, hands, ingredients, how a dish is made. Needs real photos/video from the kitchen | Photo post / Reel |
| **4. Temporada y clima** | 10% | Cold day → caldos; hot day → aguas frescas; back to school; payday. Reacts to the calendar | Headline + dish |
| **5. Comunidad y gente** | 10% | The team, regulars, reviews (with permission), "tag a quien invitarías" | Photo / quote card |
| **6. Útil** | 10% | Hours, location in the mall, how to order, what's on the breakfast menu. Always accurate **[confirm hours/menu]** | Solid color, text-heavy |

Rules: never invent prices, discounts or hours; each post has one clear idea and one invitation
("pásale", "te esperamos en Plaza Juárez", "etiqueta a quien te acompañaría").

## 4. Weekly rhythm

| Day | Slot | Default pillar |
|---|---|---|
| Tue | noon | Plato estrella (lunch crowd) |
| Thu | noon | Ritual / temporada |
| Sat | noon | Detrás del sabor or Comunidad (weekend families) |

Review after 8 weeks. If a pillar's posts get consistently fewer saves/shares, shrink it.

## 5. Calendar: dates worth a post (Mexico)

Already encoded in the draft generator: Candelaria (Feb 2, tamales), Amor y Amistad (Feb 14), Día del Niño (Apr 30),
Cinco de Mayo, Día de las Madres (May 10), Día del Padre (3rd Sunday of June), Fiestas Patrias (Sep 15–16),
Día de Muertos (Nov 2), Virgen de Guadalupe (Dec 12), Navidad, Fin de año.

Biggest restaurant days: **Día de las Madres** and **Día del Padre** (family meals). Plan these 2–3 weeks ahead:
1. Teaser post ~10 days before ("¿Ya sabes dónde vas a comer con mamá?").
2. Hero post 2–3 days before.
3. Day-of post, warm greeting. Only mention a special menu/reservation if one really exists **[confirm]**.

## 6. Promotions (when they exist)

- Add each promo to `content/promos.json` (id, text, from, to). The generator posts it automatically, with real dates.
- Max 1 promo post per 4 days; promos should be ~20% of posts so the feed doesn't feel like a flyer.
- Post the promo again with a countdown on its last day (Stories).

## 7. Beyond the feed (free, high impact)

- **Google Business Profile** for Plaza Juárez: photos, hours, menu, reply to every review. This drives more walk-ins than Instagram.
- **Stories** daily: repost the feed post, add a poll ("¿caldo o chilaquiles?"). Stories need no pipeline.
- **Reels** 1–2 a month: sizzle/pour/cheese-pull clips, 7–10 seconds, no talking.
- **WhatsApp Business**: one link in bio for orders/questions **[confirm]**.
- **Highlights**: Menú, Ubicación, Reseñas.
- Reply to comments within a day. Ask a question in half the captions to invite replies.

## 8. What we measure (monthly, 15 minutes)
- Saves and shares per post (best signal of "I want to go there").
- Profile visits → website clicks → "Cómo llegar" taps on Google.
- Follower growth in Ciudad Juárez.
- Which pillar and which dish win. Double down on the top 3 dishes.

## 9. Content production checklist
- Photos: whole plate in frame, plain background, natural light, rim fully visible (cutouts depend on it).
- 5 more hero dishes needed (list in `content/dishes-cutout/_needs-new-photo/`).
- 10 kitchen / team photos for pillar 3 and 5.
- A short list of 3–5 real customer quotes (with permission).

## 10. First 30 days
1. Week 1: finish templates, load 20 captions, approve the first 3 posts.
2. Week 2: set up Google Business Profile + Highlights.
3. Week 3: first ritual ("Martes de caldo") and first Reel.
4. Week 4: review numbers, adjust pillar mix, write next batch of captions.
